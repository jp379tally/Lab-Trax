The app currently supports multiple 2FA channels, including SMS, but SMS depends on an external vendor/integration that is not reliable or not available. Keeping SMS paths active creates broken authentication flows, failed verification attempts, confusing UI, and possible account access issues.

All two-factor authorization flows should work through email verification only. Users should no longer see SMS as an option, be prompted to enter a phone number for 2FA, receive SMS verification instructions, or hit backend SMS send/receive logic.
